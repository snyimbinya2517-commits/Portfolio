## Entrepreneurial WIL Project

Our team set out to design a community-focused business solution for the sanitation problem in Khayelitsha, Cape Town: frequent sewage pipe bursts and blocked drains that leave households, schools and small businesses without clean water, sometimes for days. Our proposed service is a Sewage Repair and Monitoring business for Khayelitsha residents and the surrounding municipality.

The team has five members: Liyema Mangaliso (Team Leader), Talent Nocuze (Researcher and Agile Lead), Sinethemba Nyimbinya (Writer and Editor, my role), Muso Nkuntsu (Presenter and Analyst), and Libolwethu Nokenke (Designer and Developer).

### Agile Team Charter

**Evidence**

Our charter was agreed and submitted on 4 May 2026. It covers:

- **Purpose and vision:** to deliver a researched, community-backed business proposal for a Sewage Repair and Monitoring service, with success measured by survey-backed research, a value proposition that fits the real problem, and on-time submission.
- **Roles:** each member has defined responsibilities, from coordination and sprint goals (Team Leader) to writing and editing (my role), analysis and presenting, and design and formatting.
- **Ways of working:** Kanban on Trello, a 10-minute daily stand-up on WhatsApp, weekly planning, a review before each submission, and a retrospective at the end of each phase.
- **Working agreements and values:** arrive prepared, respect timeboxes, give constructive feedback, raise blockers early, and commit to continuous improvement. Our values are respect, transparency, accountability, trust and a learning mindset.
- **Communication:** WhatsApp for daily contact, Google Meet or Teams for meetings, Google Drive for shared documents, and email for lecturers. Response times were set at 3 hours for normal messages and 1 hour for urgent ones.
- **Decision-making:** team vote.
- **Definition of Done:** content edited by the Writer, design checked, research verified, slides rehearsed, Team Leader sign-off, uploaded to Drive, and submitted before the deadline.
- **Risks:** clashing schedules and part-time work, limited data and connectivity, low survey response rates, and limited business-planning experience.
